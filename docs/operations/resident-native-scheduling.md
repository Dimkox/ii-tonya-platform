# Native resident scheduling

Native scheduling uses the existing expert work bot, canonical services, slots and reservations. It is available only through the existing enabled expert-card and booking capabilities; no provider activation, new public route or production enablement is part of this change.

An owner uses `/slots_setup` to choose a named timezone, dates (at most 31 days), weekdays, working hours, breaks and buffer. Each answer is explicitly entered with `/slots_answer`. `/slots_import` accepts pasted text or CSV within the existing 4000-character Telegram command limit; the underlying importer additionally rejects more than 64 KB or 500 lines. `/slots_preview` shows all intervals, omissions and errors, with bound page/confirmation commands. Every page must be acknowledged. Editing parameters or the source card invalidates an old preview. `/schedule` remains the existing appointment query; ordinary profile text, photos, documents and tag replies retain their own routes.

Before the first card approval, this saves a private schedule included in full owner and manager review. The review digest and immutable approved card bind the exact confirmed schedule. Approval creates owner-scoped service identities and versioned availability, but current paid coverage, consent, approval, publication and safety still govern discovery and booking. The migration also maps existing latest approved rich services without requiring another card review.

For already approved services, `/slots_setup` updates free availability immediately after owner confirmation. It does not reopen moderation or change custom-tag decisions. Use `/slots_card` to prepare a schedule against a changed draft's services for its new card review. New service content, duration or logistics cannot become public before approval. A content change clears a private schedule bound to the previous draft. An approval without a newly confirmed schedule withdraws old free availability; the owner can then confirm a schedule against the newly approved services. No unreviewed intervals or recurring extension are silently created.

`/slots_busy YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM-HH:MM` marks an external busy interval within the configured horizon. It hides free offers and reports existing requests/meetings for human action. It does not cancel an appointment or perform an external calendar operation. Generation, import and busy changes serialize with booking through the canonical expert row and advisory barrier. Reservations also retain the existing ordered subject/client/slot locks. Expert occupancy includes the approved buffer across native and external service alternatives; client occupancy uses the actual appointment interval.

Native slots have explicit confirmation/version/horizon authority and no fabricated provider observation. External observations keep their 15-minute freshness rule. Protected choices keep the 24-hour lead and are bounded by request expiry and the confirmed native horizon. Admission is rechecked using database time after lock waits. Voluntary pause or unpaid expiry immediately hides new availability, while already booked appointments retain their approved details and authorized cancellation/read paths under current privacy and safety rules.

Native confirmation inserts a validated immutable snapshot and books locally. It does not insert Planerka operations. The original callback, audit and normal notification worker retain their replay/recipient protections. Native cancellation uses ordinary appointment language; external calendar uncertainty semantics are unchanged.

## Private logistics and recovery

The ordinary booking-notification runtime also polls the dedicated `resident_native_logistics` ledger. Offline address, arrival instructions and approved map may be sent after confirmation. Online room links are due only within 30 minutes before the appointment and before its start. These messages use the immutable booked snapshot and the original client bot/identity/consent; arbitrary URLs are not admitted into generic notification views.

Each send commits a unique claim before network work, then holds canonical recipient, consent, reservation and bot-control authority through the bounded wire attempt. Cancellation, identity replacement, consent withdrawal, erasure and safety restrictions suppress disclosure. Pause/expiry of a listing does not require another payment to honor an existing meeting. Bot pause and throttling apply to both delivery paths. Shutdown stops new native sends; recovery-only operation can recover abandoned claims without sending.

An abandoned or uncertain attempt becomes `unknown` and is never automatically resent. A known original transport receipt can be recorded through the internal `resolveReceipt` operation after a lost database acknowledgment; it must match the original bot, chat and attempt. Do not fabricate a receipt or reset unknown work to pending. An explicit `not_applied` outcome may retry at most three attempts with the recorded throttle. Erasure deletes native client confirmation/logistics data; owner erasure also deletes private schedules, raw imports, service versions and retained canonical service labels.

## Migration and rollback

Migration 0070 is hand-authored and append-only relative to 0070's predecessors. Apply it using the repository's migration workflow. Do not run `drizzle-kit generate` or edit 0043/0069. State was reconstructed by full SQL replay and fresh-directory introspection on the task-owned PostgreSQL 17.5 cluster, with exact SQL-only functions, triggers and constraints retained separately.

Take the normal database backup before an authorized deployment. A source rollback that loses native confirmation/read/logistics support is unsafe once native bookings exist. Stop new booking admission and new sends using the existing reviewed controls, preserve booked snapshots and unknown delivery facts, and roll forward with a reviewed correction. Do not drop native tables, relabel booked rows, invent provider success, or overwrite held/booked slot identities as a rollback technique. Restoring listing eligibility never extends the owner's confirmed dates.

Local evidence is synthetic and does not prove live provider delivery, hosted CI or release readiness. The implementation evidence manifest records the executed checks and their failed predecessors. Independent review and the coordinator's exact-head gate remain delivery prerequisites.
